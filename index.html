How to Play Car Dodge? - Complete Guide

Car Dodge is a thrilling endless racing game developed by FunBit Games Studio in Chakan, Pune, Maharashtra, India. The game is inspired by classic top-down racing games but with modern realistic cars like in your screenshot!

The objective is simple: Survive as long as possible without crashing! Your blue car is at the bottom, orange and red enemy cars come from top at high speed. You must dodge them by moving left and right.

Controls:

  • On PC: Use LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys, or A and D keys, or drag mouse on road.
  • On Mobile: Drag your finger on the road to move car, or use LEFT / RIGHT buttons below game.
  • Collect points automatically - 1 point per frame you survive. Speed increases every 50 points!

Features: Realistic cars with headlights and windshield, moving road lines for real driving feel, 3-lane highway, high score saved in browser, works offline after load, no login required, 100% free and safe for kids.

We built this game for fun and to learn web development. It is lightweight (only 30KB), loads fast even on 2G internet, and does not collect any personal data. Perfect for time-pass during travel!
